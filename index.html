The hand harvest
June — September
When sun and wind agree
01 / GUIDE

Let the sea in.

Atlantic water travels through a patient network of channels and settles into shallow clay œillets, warmed by the day.

02 / WAIT

Read the surface.

Wind lifts the water. Sun concentrates it. A fine skin starts to gather — a brief, bright signal that the pond is ready.

03 / RAKE

Draw it ashore.

With a wooden las and a lousse à fleur, we gather each salt by hand. The colour, texture and flavour remain exactly of this place.
